# Extracted from

`mcp-writecheck` ports pieces of IsoKron's internal audit toolkit rather than rewriting them, following the extraction list in the design (section 10.1). Every ported file is listed here with its source path and the commit it was taken from. What the design keeps private was not ported: the AI agents, refutation, the evidence chain and its anchors, the runner (authorization, sandbox lease, stages), the per-platform adapters, the TLS fault proxy's kubeconfig part, the SSRF probe class, the keyed body commitment, and the statistics beyond one interval.

Source: IsoKron's internal audit toolkit, commit `fa7b80572a0b183c1dd85e1fa578c302322c46ba` (13 Nov 2026), for every row. The paths in the second column are paths inside that toolkit; it is not public.

While porting, comments and identifiers that named internal ticket numbers, private decisions, vendors, AI models or audit findings were removed or made generic; the published-case citations belong in the spec, not in the code.
